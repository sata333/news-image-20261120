## 2. Source Fact-Checking & Verification Details

### 1. Shenzhen Trilateral Summit (Trump, Xi, Putin)
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Held on the sidelines of the 33rd APEC Summit in Shenzhen at Kylin Villa. First trilateral summit between Chinese President Xi Jinping, US President Donald Trump, and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Joint declaration on strategic stability and peace issued, but lacked substantive ceasefire commitments on Ukraine.
- **Sources:** Chinese MFA (`fmprc.gov.cn`, `www.gov.cn`), White House (`whitehouse.gov`), Kremlin (`kremlin.ru`), The Guardian, CNN, CFR.

### 2. ICJ Genocide Ruling on Myanmar
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** The International Court of Justice (ICJ) in The Hague delivered a landmark ruling holding Myanmar directly responsible for committing genocide against the Rohingya minority in Rakhine State under the 1948 Genocide Convention.
- **Sources:** Global Issues, UN News, ICJ official releases.

### 3. Kyiv Air Strikes & Crimea Bridge Aftermath
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Following Ukraine's Nov 17 missile strike collapsing a road span of the Kerch Strait Bridge, Russia launched massive retaliatory missile/drone waves on Nov 19-20. Targets in Kyiv included three Dnipro river bridges (Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Southern, Darnytskyi), near foreign embassies, and historic St. Volodymyr's Cathedral.
- **Sources:** Kyiv Independent, CNN, Ukranews, General Staff of Armed Forces of Ukraine.

### 4. Global AI Tech Stocks Rout
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Following Anthropic's record Nasdaq IPO debut on Nov 19 ($83 IPO price, traded up 17%), shares slumped 9-14% on Nov 20. Tech sell-off cascaded worldwide: Nasdaq Composite lost >5%, Nikkei 225 tumbled over 5% (dropping below 57,000), Kospi (-5.5%), and Taiex (-6.2%).
- **Sources:** The Guardian, Al Jazeera, Anthropic IR, Vantage Markets, XTB.

### 5. NASA Voyager 1 One Light-Day Distance
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft surpassed 1 light-day (approx. 16.1 billion miles / 25.9 billion km) from Earth. Signal one-way travel time reached 24 hours (48-hour round trip). Widely commemorated by NASA JPL with public events Nov 17-20.
- **Sources:** NASA JPL, CNN, Space.com, ScienceDaily.

### 6. COP31 Antalya Climate Talks Deadlock
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Scheduled to conclude on Friday Nov 20 in Antalya, Türkiye, talks plunged into overtime after the Antalya Declaration draft deleted language calling for a transition away / phase-out of fossil fuels, sparking protests from over 30 vulnerable & EU nations.
- **Sources:** The Guardian, Climate Action Network, UN Climate Press, Al Jazeera.

### 7. Cyclone Akvan Landfall in India
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crossed the Odisha/North Andhra Pradesh coast near Gopalpur on the morning of Nov 20 with sustained winds of 70-80 km/h gusting to 90 km/h. Massive advance evacuation prevented major casualties.
- **Sources:** Indian Meteorological Department (IMD), NDTV, The Indian Express, The Hindu.

### 8. Riyadh Ballistic Missile Interceptions
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Saudi Royal Air Defense intercepted multiple ballistic missiles and suicide drones launched by Houthi forces targeting Riyadh (including the Public Investment Fund tower district) and southern Jazan region.
- **Sources:** Arab News, Jewish Rhody, House of Saud, Malay Mail.

### 9. Kenya Ebola Patient Discharge & 42-Day Countdown
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** Kenya discharged its last remaining Ebola patient and cleared all quarantined contacts. Health authorities and WHO commenced the official 42-day countdown toward declaring the country outbreak-free on Dec 23, 2026.
- **Sources:** WHO African Region, Ministry of Health Kenya, UN News.

### 10. US - Latin America Defense & Security Cooperation
- **Status:** **VERIFIED**
- **Details:** US Secretary of Defense visited Ecuador and Honduras on Nov 20 to finalize regional security, maritime interdiction, and counter-narcotics cooperation frameworks.
- **Sources:** CEPR, BNAmericas, Regional Wire.
